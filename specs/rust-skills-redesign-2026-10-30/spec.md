## Outcome and boundary

Deliver a coherent Rust instruction system intended to contribute useful
engineering judgment beyond the model's existing knowledge, together with
matching format, routing and existing harness controls, checked examples and a
future evaluation plan. Keep quality tied to each task's accepted behavior and
constraints. Finish this stage with mechanical consistency checks and independent
review. Do not perform comparative runs or implement an experimental runner,
evaluator or comparative fixture suites. No measured improvement, preferred
winner or universal quality claim is required or established by this stage.

Changes cover the Rust decision skills, their necessary supporting materials,
canonical policy references, catalog selection, structural validation and
generated carriers. Preserve service runtime behavior, supported profiles,
security and authority policy, release gates and unrelated workflow meaning.
Do not change the old comparison bundle or unrelated work. Implementation may
update existing owners where the new instruction architecture requires it;
it must not duplicate their policy elsewhere or infer authority for remote or
production effects.

## R1. Every instruction has one justified responsibility

Inventory the current Rust methods and relevant policy/control statements with
their baseline identities. Give each materially affected statement a keep,
move, merge, revise or remove disposition and a reason grounded in accepted
intent. Trace mandatory boundaries to their retained canonical owners. An
instruction guaranteed elsewhere, an apparent code fact or duplicated policy
must not survive solely to fill a format budget. Preserve genuinely useful
methods and present constraints rather than delete by name, size or skill count.

The resulting architecture distinguishes transferable engineering judgment,
conditional domain methods, version-sensitive library mechanisms, service-local
policy and workflow control. Essential authority and safety constraints remain
effective even when an optional skill is not selected. Code, contracts and
tests remain factual authorities; a pedagogical example or copied architecture
description does not replace them.

Nearest falsifier: a representative route receives contradictory policies,
cannot locate its decision owner, or loses a mandatory boundary when a skill
is omitted. Mere reduction in words or catalog entries cannot establish R1.

## R2. Methods support consequential engineering choices

The system helps agents preserve observable promises and invariants; choose
data representations and validation boundaries; assign mutation authority and
effects; reason about memory ownership and resource/task lifetime; justify the
level of abstraction; and choose evidence that distinguishes relevant failure.
It treats these as tools for unresolved choices, without imposing a reasoning
transcript, questionnaire, new artifact or extra workflow on every small edit.

Recommendations include their causal reason, applicability, meaningful tradeoff
and condition that changes the choice. Do not promote spellings or counts into
universal quality rules: a clone, trait with one implementation, plain DTO,
loop, public field or helper can be justified by the task. Types protect the
invariants they actually encode; all construction and mutation paths matter,
and dynamic or cross-resource invariants may need runtime checks. An expired
wait does not establish stopped work or absence of a durable effect. Simplicity
includes understanding, use and real change cost; line count is diagnostic.

The design-related content of `coder`, `idiomatic` and `structural-quality`
receives one coherent ownership decision. Failure/outcome semantics retain a
distinct purpose. Lifetime/cancellation and budget/retry methods remain
distinguishable. Test construction and evidence sufficiency remain
distinguishable; diagnosis and measurement retain their independent questions.
Client contracts and library mechanisms retain separate authority. Other
Rust methods are retained, reorganized or removed only with a recorded present
reason. Final names, entrypoint count and file allocation belong to Design.

Nearest falsifier: two superficially similar tasks with different invariants
receive the same blanket prescription, or a method creates structure that
does not carry any current constraint. Preserve cases where the existing
small solution already satisfies the task.

## R3. Format and examples communicate choice boundaries

Permit concise code, lists, links and conditional supporting references when
they clarify a consequential decision. Remove the minimum word count and
mandatory paragraph rituals. Keep essential distinctions in the loaded method;
conditional detail must have a discoverable, discriminating trigger. Account
for the complete reading route rather than equating a short entry file with
low context cost. Match validation and generated carriers to the accepted
format without loosening unrelated workflow or license checks.

Examples state the relevant premise, preserved property, cost and changed
condition that reverses the recommendation. Use compact contrasts where they
teach a boundary and precise existing-code links where they support local
application. A repository link is evidence to inspect, not an automatic gold
standard. Check compilable examples under the selected compatible toolchain
and dependencies; label deliberate fragments or non-compiling contrasts
accurately. Their technical validity does not establish educational benefit.

Nearest falsifier: an example encourages a wrong decision on its stated
boundary case, a required distinction is hidden in optional reading, or the
checker rejects the intended format while accepting stale generated output.

## R4. Selection is based on the unresolved decision

Descriptions and routing make neighboring methods distinguishable, including
negative and overlapping cases. Mention of Rust syntax or a library alone is
not sufficient evidence that every related method is needed. Multiple methods
may apply when they answer different material questions. Simple tasks can need
no additional method. Role/workflow carriers retain their existing invocation
and permission meaning.

Every asserted guaranteed-loading path needs an implementation-level delivery
receipt; a description or instruction to read is not that receipt. The system
must not silently treat missing required content as loaded or allow a domain
method to redefine the enclosing workflow's completion policy.

Nearest falsifier: a small edit attracts unrelated methods, a lifetime/budget
case loses one necessary method, or recorded content differs from the package
that the evaluation labels as supplied.

## R5. The future evaluation plan separates four claims

Document a future evaluation protocol for the following questions without
implementing or running its experiment in this stage:

| Question | Required comparison and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Content | Supply complete prespecified packages for the same tasks: no additional Rust package, current baseline and revised system. Hold model, effort, common mandatory policy, tools, source inputs and execution budget fixed. BeautifulRust may be an additional reference arm; it cannot replace the no-package baseline. |
| Examples | Compare the same principles with and without the examples independently of whole-system changes. Account for additional explanation/content so an observed effect is not automatically attributed to code formatting. |
| Selection | Evaluate catalog-driven selection separately on positive, negative and overlapping new cases. Allow multiple justified sets; measure missing and excess methods and selection cost. |
| End-to-end | Run normal bounded agent work with real selection, context acquisition, allowed validation and continuation. Report the outcome of the integrated system separately from supplied-content experiments. |

The plan requires a fixed protocol, candidate identities, common context,
conditions, task allocation, limits, stop/recovery rules and decision criteria
before a later run. It preserves every started run, prevents uncontrolled
alternate access to experimental content and separates fixed references from
an explicitly identified retrieval condition. It does not invent a current
sample size or live model budget as a prerequisite for local completion.

The documented evidence requirements cover supplied bytes and hashes,
input/package identities, effective model and settings, tools, timings, usage,
candidate artifacts and terminal outcomes. Distinguish full request/context
evidence from merely passing text to a runner. Current native CLI and historical
receipts do not establish observable assembled-request bytes or retained model
context. The future protocol must qualify exposure at the capability actually
available; it must not promise those unseen surfaces. Account for truncation or
compaction before claiming continued presence. Supply does not prove cognition.
Unavailable exposure evidence narrows or invalidates the affected claim under
the later frozen rule, never a fabricated pass or selectively discarded outcome.

Use the existing historical reports only as leads. Neither their narrow
samples nor post-inspection probes become a confirmatory new holdout.
Nearest falsifier: a content condition contains optional discovery, another
arm's content leaks into the common context, or a whole-system difference is
reported as the effect of examples alone.

## R6. The future plan preserves diverse tasks and blind assessment

The documented future design separates development/pilot tasks from a closed
confirmatory holdout before candidate inspection. Its task families cover
modeling/parsing/identity/order, library API and internal application code,
behavior-preserving changes, concurrency and
lifetime, durable outcomes and retry, integration/configuration/HTTP,
diagnosis/measurement and simple tasks where guidance may be unnecessary.
It includes realistic changes spanning files and subsequent changes performed
by fresh executors. Resume/continuation, when studied, is a different condition
from a new session receiving the changed source. The accepted 8–12-task pilot
recommendation is future method calibration, not a current execution unit.
A later authorized evaluation selects the wider holdout and repetitions from
decision precision, execution cost and observed variance rather than treating
a fixed sample count as proof of general superiority. Do not create the new
experimental task suites now.

The future protocol freezes original requirements, correctness checks and
assessment criteria before viewing candidate output. Correctness and task
constraints are reported separately from maintainability preference. New
post-inspection probes remain exploratory and can enter a later unseen set.
Blind assessors receive task, baseline and anonymous candidate artifacts without
arm labels, skill text or identifying logs. It uses independent assessors,
randomized order, ties and retained disagreements, and records blinding limits.

The future report separates clarity, idiomatic fit, justified complexity,
subsequent-change cost, regressions, time and token use. Repetitions are not independent new
tasks. Analyze by tasks and families, include uncertainty, worst regressions,
infrastructure failures and all started outcomes under the prespecified rule.
Preserve retention cases for adequate existing solutions, small-change
restraint, user corrections/continuation and legitimate scope or authority
stops. Do not require the system to override a justified stop to improve a
completion score.

Nearest falsifier: a preferred candidate violates a task requirement, assessors
can infer arms from supplied metadata, a later probe changes the original
correctness verdict, or an average hides a material family-level regression.

## R7. Local delivery is reversible and claims stay bounded

Implement one coherent core and align its format controls, then apply the
accepted architecture across all current Rust methods, including the explicit
disposition of every method retained, merged or removed. Do not leave a
prototype-only delivery awaiting a pilot. Static consistency and independent
review can complete this stage; a future behavioral comparison is not its gate.
The architecture remains provisional with respect to behavioral improvement.
Preserve exact baseline and candidate identities so instruction
rollback is recoverable without rewriting historical evidence. Record future
refresh triggers for material model, harness or relevant library changes.
Reducing unsupported instruction is a valid outcome; no general quality claim
follows from a shorter catalog, checked examples or a reviewer verdict.

The deliverable includes the revised instruction system, consistent existing
controls and references, technically checked examples, the future evaluation
plan and actual results of applicable existing instruction checks and final
independent review. Static checks prove structure and consistency only. Do not
mark deferred experiments as missing current acceptance or create an evaluation
environment to close the local work. A genuine in-scope defect still requires
repair; an unavailable mandatory local check is reported with its exact scope.
